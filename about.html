Profile

My practice begins with things that have been used, handled, corrected, folded, or left behind: artist books, old tickets, practice papers, printed matter, and small technical interfaces.

I collect, sequence, fold, write, and test these materials, allowing their traces and unfinished states to remain visible. Across paper, objects, archives, and responsive fields, I am interested in how memory and pressure take form, and how a material can continue to speak after its original function has faded.

My current work moves between book-based spatial structures, embodied writing, everyday archives, and interactive fields.

Materials

Artist books, found tickets, practice papers, printed matter, ink, and responsive interfaces.

Processes

Collecting, sequencing, folding, writing, binding, archiving, and testing.

Questions within the practice

What remains when a system no longer performs its original function?

How can traces, residues, and atmospheres become perceptual structures?

How can material and technical systems be translated without reducing them to representation?
