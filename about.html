Profile

I am an artist and designer based in Shenzhen and Hong Kong. My work moves across drawing, collected printed matter, artists’ books and interactive forms. I use materials, images, page structures and digital tools to explore different ways of reading, handling and participating in a work.

Alongside my own practice, I have worked in art and design teaching, project mentoring and educational programme development. These experiences have shaped my interest in how people explore possibilities, respond to feedback and develop their own judgements. I am also interested in how teaching can support initiative and experimentation while leaving room for learners to take a project in an unexpected direction.

My current projects include investigations of collected commercial papers, books made from traces of writing, and interactive digital works. Physical making and digital experimentation play different roles across these projects. I document what I make, the decisions involved and the questions that remain open.
