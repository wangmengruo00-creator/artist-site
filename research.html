Research Statement

My practice investigates how printed matter, image structures, and embodied reading can hold memory, spatial order, and perceptual change.

Current Research Questions

How can artist books and image-based systems become methods for studying material memory, tacit knowledge, and self-transformation through making?

  • Material Memory and Tacit Knowledge
  • Image-based Research and Self-Transformation
  • Spatial-Sensory Field
