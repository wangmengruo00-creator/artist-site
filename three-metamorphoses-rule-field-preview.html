Limits / open questions

Repetition is inferred from spatial overlap with earlier traces, while novelty means entering cells not yet visited in this session. These are operational definitions, not psychological facts. Screen size, pointer hardware, ambient light and familiarity with drawing interfaces can all change the result.

The camera reads only 48 × 36 luminance differences. Movement and brightness can be confounded, access is uneven across devices, and opting out must remain a complete version of the work. Nietzsche’s sequence is culturally and historically situated; this project’s coexistence model is an artistic reinterpretation rather than a universal account of transformation.

Sound is intentionally absent from the current prototype: it might make duration and accumulation more bodily, but it could also overstate a field designed to remain quiet and unresolved. A later comparison should test sparse sonic feedback as a temporal layer rather than assume that another medium automatically deepens the work.

The computation is exposed so that it can be questioned. The person remains unresolved.

01 / Project position and development

A field shaped by movement, stillness and what remains.

Core proposition

The work does not illustrate transformation as a sequence. It builds a field in which carrying, resisting and beginning can overlap and be felt without being named.

Nietzsche’s chapter “On the Three Metamorphoses” in Thus Spoke Zarathustra gave language to changes I recognised from lived experience. It prompted a further question: if a philosophical text can reorganise one reader’s perception, can an interactive field allow transformation to be encountered before it is explained?

Personal resonance / not autobiography

I had experienced carrying, resisting and becoming as real changes, but not as clean or dramatic stages. Even rupture did not feel like a spectacular collision. It appeared gradually and naturally, overlapping with responsibility, hesitation, curiosity and the making of new possibilities.

The work therefore does not retell my biography or assume that every visitor shares the same story. It uses that experience to ask whether subtle internal change can become perceptible without being explained, diagnosed or represented literally.

Research contribution

Rather than translating Nietzsche’s three figures into images, the project draws from bearing, refusal and beginning again as provisional forces that can overlap, recur and remain unfinished within the field.

Two inputs / two kinds of presence
Mouse / intentional movement

Precise, directional, trace-making

Movement establishes direction and fine trace. Repetition builds local density; abrupt changes introduce friction; exploratory paths open unused space.

Optional camera / coarse visual change

Atmospheric, frame-based, less precise

If the visitor actively enables it, low-resolution luminance differences detect coarse movement and brightness change in the camera frame. These signals affect global pressure, breathing and instability differently from the mouse.

Current camera test / reflection

The camera was introduced to test a kind of presence that is atmospheric rather than directional. In the current prototype, larger changes in the frame intensify bright points and can produce an expansive burst.

That response may be too explosive for the project’s quieter logic. The next iteration will test global pressure, breathing and brief instability without treating the camera as another drawing instrument or assigning meaning to the body it senses.

Camera ethicsOpt-in only. A 48 × 36 luminance sample is processed locally and temporarily; no image is displayed, stored or transmitted, and no identity recognition is used. The field remains usable without camera access.

Development trajectory

The project began as a non-interactive particle state. Through iterative web experiments, interaction entered gradually: first through mouse direction and trace, then through optional low-resolution camera sensing as an attempt to introduce a different kind of movement, rhythm and breath.

Particles, diffusion and water-like movement were chosen because the three forces felt microscopic, elusive and continuously flowing. Brighter halos, decorative light and firework-like effects were tested and rejected: their visual impact made transformation appear more violent and theatrical than it felt in lived experience.

The field does not tell the viewer what state they are in. It changes with the way they enter it.

03 / Spiritual interface prototype

A physical threshold, not a remote control.

The M5Stack Cardputer ADV tests what changes when a philosophical encounter becomes handheld, private and intentional. Its small display, physical keys and intervals of waiting create a threshold before the viewer enters the perceptual field.

Here “spiritual interface” means a reflective, philosophical threshold rather than a religious claim or psychological instrument. Entering a name is a temporary gesture of arrival. Randomised prompts and variable responses prevent a single prescribed path, but the interface does not score, diagnose or tell the participant which metamorphosis they are.
