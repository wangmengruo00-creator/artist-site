ONGOING RESEARCH PROJECT / 2026–

The Three Metamorphoses / 精神三变

Ongoing research · Two separate technical prototypes

Taking Nietzsche’s camel, lion and child as prompts rather than images to illustrate, The Three Metamorphoses asks whether transformation can be translated into movement, duration and response.

Early studies shifted from symbolic figures towards accumulation, disturbance and unstable fields. The project is now being tested through a responsive webpage and a standalone M5Stack Cardputer prototype. They are not yet connected.

01

INSPIRATION AND PERSONAL REFLECTION

Reading Nietzsche alongside personal reflection moved the project away from illustrating three figures. Transformation is approached instead through traces that accumulate, disturbances that alter a condition, and fields that remain unresolved.

02 / PROTOTYPE A

RESPONSIVE WEBPAGE PROTOTYPE

A browser-based study of how pointer movement, stillness and duration leave temporary traces.

Move / pause / stay · Responsive webpage prototype

03 / PROTOTYPE B

STANDALONE M5STACK CARDPUTER PROTOTYPE

The standalone M5Stack Cardputer tests a small handheld interaction through short inputs and returned messages. It was developed separately from the responsive webpage and is not currently connected to it.

The screen visible behind the device is part of the test documentation, not a live connection.

PROPOSED INTEGRATION · FUTURE INTERACTION LOGIC

A possible relationship between the two prototypes

Concept visualisations for a possible future spatial integration. The interaction logic and technical connection remain under development.

04

CURRENT STATUS

Two separate technical prototypes are currently being tested. Their future integration remains under development.
