The Three Metamorphoses / 精神三变

A responsive field of burden, rupture, and becoming.

The Three Metamorphoses draws on Nietzsche's metaphors of the camel, lion, and child, as well as my own reflections on growth and transformation. Rather than treating them as three successive stages, the work understands burden, rupture, and becoming as forces that continuously coexist and overlap.

The project began as an interactive web field composed of blurred traces, residual images, and shifting densities. Movement, stillness, direction, and bodily presence disturb the field and leave temporary visual residues. The system does not classify the viewer or offer a conclusion; it reflects moments of action, hesitation, and pause.

  • Move to write memory.
  • Stay to let the field breathe.
  • Let your presence disturb what remains.

Physical Interface Prototype

A handheld point of entry

The M5Stack Cardputer ADV functions as a handheld interface prototype. A name or brief input becomes a small act of entry—not identification or diagnosis, but a way of establishing a personal and physical relationship with the field.

Installation Proposal

A spatial threshold

Concept visualization, 2026

The installation proposal translates the web-based field into a spatial threshold. Movement, stillness, distance, and bodily presence become forms of input, while layered projections, blurred traces, and residual images respond to the viewer and remain briefly after their departure.

Project Note

The work does not ask the viewer to identify or complete three distinct stages. It offers a field that can be entered, disturbed, and marked—a temporary reflection of one's own state rather than an explanation of spiritual transformation.
