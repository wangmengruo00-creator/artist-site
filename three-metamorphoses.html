The Three Metamorphoses / 精神三变

A perceptual field of burden, rupture, and becoming.

An interactive web field about how inner states transform through body, perception, and time.

The work begins from Nietzsche's camel, lion, and child, but it does not explain the philosophical text or visualize the three figures directly. Instead, it translates them into recurring forces: burden, rupture, and becoming.

《精神三变》是一个关于精神状态如何在身体、感知与时间中变形的互动网页场域。 作品从尼采的“骆驼、狮子、孩童”出发，但并不将它们作为具象符号呈现， 而是转译为三种反复出现的精神力量：负重、冲破与生成。

鼠标移动留下记忆般的痕迹，摄像头感应身体带来的扰动， 静止则让残留缓慢沉积、扩散和呼吸。

  • Move to write memory.
  • Stay to let the field breathe.
  • Let your presence disturb what remains.

Project Note

The Three Metamorphoses does not function as a test, a stage-selection system, or an explanation of Nietzsche. It uses the idea of metamorphosis as a method of self-translation, turning responsibility, expectation, resistance, and regeneration into a field that can be entered and disturbed.

Mouse movement leaves directional memory traces; bodily presence sensed through the camera introduces vibration, rupture, and slight disturbance; stillness allows residues to accumulate, diffuse, and fade. The viewer does not receive a result. Transformation appears as residue, density, breathing, and fading traces.

《精神三变》不是我对尼采的解释，而是我借由“精神三变”找到的一种自我转译方法， 并将这种转译开放为一个他人可以进入的感知场。
