ONGOING RESEARCH / INTERACTION + INTERFACE / 2026–

The Three Metamorphoses

Nietzsche’s camel, lion and child are treated as prompts rather than images to illustrate. The project asks whether philosophical transformation can be encountered through movement, waiting, language and response.

01 / SOURCE → TRANSLATION

Three figures became three forces.

I began through reading and intuitive visual tests. Rather than representing three fixed characters, I translated each figure into a quality that could organise movement and response.

RULE EVIDENCE / WEB FIELD

Input → rule → state change → perceptual consequence

Encounter Rule Visible consequence
Move Movement deposits an active trace. The path extends and the field ruptures.
Pause Stillness allows residue to accumulate. Density gathers around the last position.
Stay Duration alters the field state. The image thickens, fades, or leaves an afterimage.

04 / PROTOTYPE B · PHILOSOPHICAL INTERFACE

A question held in the hand.

The Cardputer is not a controller for the field. It is a small, private interface for questioning, choosing and waiting. The final philosopher and quotation mapping will be added from the project’s source material.
