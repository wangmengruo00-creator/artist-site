01 / Research context and inquiry

How can changing relations of weight, friction and play become perceptible without defining the viewer?

The project begins from lived experiences of carrying, refusing and beginning again. Nietzsche’s “Three Metamorphoses” gave those experiences a philosophical vocabulary. Practice-based experiments then ask how that vocabulary might become perceptible as a responsive condition without becoming an image, diagnosis or identity label.

Research sequenceContext→Translation→System→Evidence→Limits→Study
Working proposition

Transformation is approached as a changing relation rather than represented as a sequence or assigned as an identity. Weight, Friction and Play are inspectable artistic variables used to construct that relation; they are not claims about a person.

Lived resonance / not autobiography

I had experienced carrying, resisting and beginning again as real changes, but not as clean or dramatic stages. Even rupture appeared gradually, overlapping with responsibility, hesitation, curiosity and the making of new possibilities.

The work does not retell my biography or assume that every visitor shares the same story. Personal experience is the origin of the question, not the content the viewer is asked to recognise.

Philosophical trigger

Nietzsche’s camel, lion and child offered three figures of transformation. This project does not illustrate them literally. It asks what happens when bearing, refusal and beginning are translated into relationships of movement, stillness, repetition and return.

The shift is from “Which stage am I in?” to “How does the field change as I enter it?”

Actual development / reconstructed history

The project did not begin as one fully planned technical system. A responsive visual field and an interest in the M5Stack Cardputer developed as adjacent experiments: one concerned traces and changing images; the other concerned holding, entering, waiting and receiving a brief response.

The current research architecture brings them into relation without claiming that they were technically connected from the beginning. This distinction between historical development and present interpretation remains visible throughout the project.

Why a web field

Hand scale / direct trace

The browser prototype isolates the smallest unit of the question: how movement, pause and return produce visible consequence.

Why a threshold

Entry scale / deliberate arrival

The handheld prototype slows the encounter down before the field begins, making entry an intentional act rather than a click.

The field does not tell the viewer what state they are in. It changes with the way they enter it.

Camel, Lion and Child inform three coexisting relations: Weight, Friction and Play.

The philosophical figures are neither illustrated nor assigned to the viewer. They inform a hand-authored analytical model through which behavioural features and perceptual consequences can be compared.

Development trajectory

The project began as a non-interactive particle state. Interaction entered gradually: first through mouse direction and trace, then through optional low-resolution camera sensing as a different kind of movement, rhythm and breath.

Particles, diffusion and water-like movement were chosen because the three forces felt microscopic, elusive and continuously flowing. Brighter halos, decorative light and firework-like effects were tested and rejected: they made transformation appear more violent and theatrical than it felt in lived experience.

Optional camera / coarse visual change

Atmospheric, frame-based, less precise

If the visitor actively enables it, low-resolution luminance differences detect coarse movement and brightness change in the camera frame. These signals affect global pressure, breathing and instability differently from the mouse.

Current camera test / reflection

The camera was introduced to test a kind of presence that is atmospheric rather than directional. In the current prototype, larger changes in the frame intensify bright points and can produce an expansive burst.

That response may be too explosive for the project’s quieter logic. The next iteration will test global pressure, breathing and brief instability without treating the camera as another drawing instrument or assigning meaning to the body it senses.

Camera ethicsOpt-in only. A 48 × 36 luminance sample is processed locally and temporarily; no image is displayed, stored or transmitted, and no identity recognition is used. The field remains usable without camera access.

05 / Limitations, ethics and epistemic boundary

The system describes traces; it does not diagnose people.

The boundary is part of the work. Every sensing and rule-making decision is paired with what the system cannot know, so the field remains open rather than becoming an instrument of classification.

Agency and boundary
The visitor chooses

Whether to move, wait, repeat, draw, enable the camera or leave.

The field receives

Movement, stillness, spatial relation and temporary low-resolution frame differences.

The field changes

Visual density, continuity, instability, diffusion and persistence.

The system cannot know

Identity, intention, emotion, belief or a person’s “true” metamorphosis.

Non-diagnosticNo personal state

Weight, Friction and Play describe relations in the current trace. They do not identify emotion, personality, intention, belief or a viewer’s “real” metamorphosis.

Camera ethicsOpt-in and local

A 48 × 36 luminance sample is processed locally and temporarily; no image is displayed, stored or transmitted, and no identity recognition is used. The field remains complete without camera access.

Operational termsDefinitions stay visible

Repetition means spatial overlap with earlier traces; novelty means entering cells not yet visited in this session. These are working definitions, not psychological facts.

Technical boundarySeparation is stated

The Web Field and M5Stack are currently separate. The proposed connection transmits only an entry-ready event, not names, prompts, answers or interpretations.

Limits / open questions

Screen size, pointer hardware, ambient light and familiarity with drawing interfaces can all change the result. The camera reads coarse luminance differences, so movement and brightness can be confounded, and access is uneven across devices.

Nietzsche’s sequence is culturally and historically situated; this coexistence model is an artistic reinterpretation rather than a universal account of transformation.

Sound is intentionally absent from the current prototype. It might make duration and accumulation more bodily, but it could also overstate a field designed to remain quiet and unresolved. A later comparison should test sparse sonic feedback as a temporal layer rather than assume another medium automatically deepens the work.

The computation is exposed so that it can be questioned. The person remains unresolved.

06 / Calibration completed → exploratory study planned

First inspect the artefact; then observe how people perceive it.

A P01 controlled calibration has been completed to test whether the programmed relations behave consistently. It revised the V1 mapping but did not study an audience. The next stage is a small qualitative pilot asking whether different field behaviours are perceptibly distinct without validating Nietzsche or scoring a person.
