Three States of Order

Beijing Order / Dragon-scale Binding

2026 / 15 × 110 cm

An artist book prototype series translating my bodily experience of Beijing’s historical order into three folded states: Placed, Remained, and Opened.

Project

Beijing Order

This project begins with my observation of Beijing as a city structured by spatial, historical, and institutional order. Instead of documenting the city directly, the work reorganises this experience through three historical image systems: The Ten Thousand Longevity Celebration, Rivers and Mountains Stretching for Thousands of Miles, and Wondrous Views of Xiao and Xiang.

The dragon-scale binding is used as a reading mechanism. Through folding, overlap, concealment, and partial visibility, continuous images are transformed into layered structures that must be entered, handled, and reassembled through time.

Reading as Structure

The work is not a travel record of Beijing, nor a redesign of historical painting. It is a material study of how historical order can be folded, retained, and opened again through the body of the reader.
