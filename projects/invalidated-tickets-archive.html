DIGITAL FIELD / 296 CANDIDATE WORKING RECORDS

The current digital field remains visible—and provisional.

An artist-led inventory identified 318 distinct physical objects. The present interface contains 185 ticket-and-voucher image records, 51 wrapper image records, 12 product-label image records, and 48 other printed-image records. What is visible, recorded, proposed, and unresolved remains deliberately separate.

These 296 candidate digital records were produced through a mixed computational and manually adjusted extraction workflow. They are not equivalent to the 318 physical objects or to completed catalogue entries; object–record correspondence is still being reconciled.
