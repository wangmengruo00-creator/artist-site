Bureau Reading System / How to read

SELECT AN OBJECT

Select a material record, then move between three reading modes. The gap between those readings is the research space of this prototype.

01

System record: what the paper may have regulated.

02

Material trace: what remains directly visible.

03

Proposed machine reading: what a formal model might connect or omit.

This is a proposed reading system, not a completed AI analysis. Machine categories remain hypotheses until they are implemented, tested, and critically compared.
