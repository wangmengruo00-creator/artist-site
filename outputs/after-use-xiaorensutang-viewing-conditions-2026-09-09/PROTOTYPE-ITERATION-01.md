# One Wrapper, Multiple Readings — Prototype Iteration 01

Date: 9 September 2026

## Status

This is an artist-operated prototype record. It is not a user study and does not test interaction effectiveness.

The prototype uses the four documented photographs and OCR records from the Xiaorensutang viewing-conditions pilot. It does not simulate illumination or create intermediate image states.

## What the artist noticed

The artist first noticed that the wrapper appeared grey and visually subdued. Her attention went primarily to its texture and material quality. Against white paper, the blue and pale areas became much stronger in contrast; the colour felt more vivid, and the image appeared more layered and three-dimensional.

## What the machine record contributed

The recognised text was broadly close to what the artist could read in several regions, but it did not help her understand the material itself. The OCR record foregrounded legible text while leaving out texture, changes in translucency and tactile differences.

The artist has not yet determined whether the machine text actively took attention away from the object. The supported conclusion at this stage is narrower: OCR did not adequately support her material reading.

## Where the prototype differs from holding the object

Switching between flat photographs with buttons cannot reproduce the continuous act of lifting the wrapper, changing its angle, holding it toward light or touching its two sides. The interface also cannot transmit the wrapper's light weight or the difference in smoothness between its surfaces.

## One design revision to test next

Move **Artist observation** beside the main image and place **Machine record** in a secondary, initially collapsed layer. The viewer should first switch between and enlarge the photographs, then open the OCR record when needed.

This revision is intended to test a presentation order, not to claim that it reproduces touch or physical handling.
