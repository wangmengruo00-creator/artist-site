Selected Works

Works

Selected works tracing a practice from artist books and printed matter toward current research on book-based structures, interactive perceptual fields, material archives, and embodied writing.

Section 01

Current Research Practice

Current PhD-related research across book-based spatial structures, interactive perceptual fields, material archives, and embodied writing systems.

01

Three States of Order

2026 Dragon-scale binding, printed matter, spatial reading structure

An artist book series translating the bodily experience of historical space into three layered states: being placed, remaining, and opening.
02

The Three Metamorphoses / 精神三变

2026– Interactive web field, visual grammar, responsive perception

A web-based perceptual field where movement, stillness, and presence accumulate as blur, rupture, density, and fading traces.
03

NOT YET

2026– Cardputer / M5Stack, handheld interface, state-based interaction

A handheld interaction prototype that uses selection, waiting, and delayed response to turn inner states into a small technological encounter.

04

Expired Ticket Bureau

2026– Old tickets, wrappers, labels, packaging, printed matter archive

An accumulating material archive reading obsolete everyday printed matter through story, loss, residue, and visual memory.

05

Draft Growth

2026– Calligraphy practice paper, ink, discarded drafts, artist book in progress

An artist book in progress using discarded calligraphy and ink practice papers as traces of bodily training, repetition, and growth.

P01

Calligraphy / Ink / Gesture Studies

P02

3D Printed Line Studies

2026– 3D print, spatial line, tactile structure, object study

P03

Old Printed Matter Collection

2026– Tickets, labels, wrappers, packaging, everyday printed matter

P04

M5Stack / Web Process

2026– Cardputer, interface testing, responsive web field, state-based interaction

Technical and interaction tests connecting small devices, waiting states, and responsive visual fields.
