Section 01

Current Practice

Four current projects through which material traces, bodily encounters, and interactive conditions are tested as distinct yet related forms of reading.

01 / Work in progress

The Bureau of Invalidated Tickets

2026– Working corpus, material archive, reading-system prototype

A working research framework within AFTER USE, bringing system records and material traces into comparison through a completed machine-assisted diagnostic reading.
02 / Current

The Three Metamorphoses

2026– Interactive web field, visual grammar, responsive perception

A web-based perceptual field where movement, stillness, and presence accumulate as blur, rupture, density, and fading traces.
03 / Current

Three States of Order (Beijing Orders)

2026 Material prototype, speculative studies, interactive binding study

Dragon-scale-bound artist books translating embodied encounters with Beijing’s architectural and historical order into layered material reading.
04 / Current

Skin of Writing

2025–present Ink, calligraphy practice paper, handmade paper, hand binding

A hand-bound book object gathering the pressure, correction, and residue of writing practice.
